What concrete services do you offer?

We provide commercial and residential concrete projects in Tauranga, including concrete driveways, foundations, house slabs, commercial slabs, patios, blockwork, coloured concrete, retaining walls and concrete repair.

Which areas do you cover?

We provide concrete services in Tauranga and the Bay of Plenty, New Zealand.

Can you repair existing concrete?

Yes. We repair cracks and spalling in concrete slabs, driveways, patios, stairs and other structures, as well as rusty discolouration, abrasions and bubbling surfaces.

We handle commercial and large residential concrete projects. For small jobs such as patios, paths, garden features, stairs and steps, please visit our Tauranga Small Concrete Jobs page linked on our home page.
