Does The Colour Of The Concrete Fade Over Time?

Concerning the colour of concrete in Tauranga, one might ask: Does it fade over time? This is an important question to consider when deciding between different types of concrete.

In general, concrete will eventually discolor and fade due to exposure to ultraviolet light from the sun or other sources like water. It’s a natural part of the aging process for any material exposed to these elements. However, there are ways to mitigate this fading effect by adding sealers or coatings that can help protect against UV rays and preserve its color longer. Additionally, some colors are more resistant than others and won’t fade as quickly. For example, darker colors have higher resistance to UV radiation; whereas lighter colors tend to degrade faster.

It’s best practice before making your decision on which type of coloured concrete you should go with in Tauranga, research into what kind of products may be available for protection against UV radiation and fading effects. By taking these measures you’ll ensure that your chosen hue remains vibrant for many years to come!

What Is The Typical Turnaround Time For A Coloured Concrete Job?

Coloured concrete is a popular choice for landscaping, driveways and patios in the Tauranga area. But when it comes to getting the job done, one of the most important questions people have is: what’s the typical turnaround time?

The answer depends on several factors. For example, if you’re pouring new concrete then curing times will need to be taken into account. If you’re resurfacing existing concrete then preparation work may take longer. The size and complexity of your project will also influence how long it takes – so small jobs can typically be finished faster than larger ones. Additionally, access constraints can cause further delays.

In general, coloured concrete projects usually take from two days up to seven or more depending on these variables. It’s always best to speak directly with your contractor about their estimated timeline for completion before starting any job.

Is The Coloured Concrete Surface Slip Resistant?

When it comes to coloured concrete surfaces, safety is often a priority. That’s why many homeowners wonder: Is the coloured concrete surface slip resistant? The answer is yes! Coloured concrete has been engineered and designed for enhanced traction – making it an ideal choice for both exterior and interior applications.

The mixture of sand and cement used in coloured concrete can be adjusted to create a non-slip texture that provides extra grip on flat surfaces. As a result, this type of surface can be safely enjoyed by everyone who uses it. Plus, you don’t have to worry about slipping or skidding due to wet spots or unexpected weather conditions.

TIP: When considering coloured concrete for your home, ask your supplier if they offer special additives that provide added traction – these are especially helpful when installing around swimming pools or other slippery areas.
